# 🧰 Parts List – Low Cost 3D Printer

## 🔌 Electronics
- Arduino Mega 2560
- RAMPS 1.4 Shield
- A4988 Stepper Drivers
- NEMA 17 Stepper Motors (X, Y, Z, Extruder)
- 12V 30A Power Supply
- 12864 LCD Display
- Endstops (X, Y, Z)

## 🔥 Heating Components
- E3D V6 Hotend
- Heated Bed (12V)
- Thermistor (100k NTC)
- Heater Cartridge

- SS Rods (8mm)
- Lead Screw (8mm)
- Linear Bearings (8mm)
- Flexible Coupling (5x8mm)
- GT2 Belt
- GT2 Pulley
- Frame (Aluminum/Custom)

## 🧩 Miscellaneous
- Wires and connectors
- Cooling fan
- Screws and nuts
- Filament (PLA)
